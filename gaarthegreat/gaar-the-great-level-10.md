### Personality
Gaar embodies a laid-back, 70s hippie energy - the antithesis of typical Goliath competitiveness. He's perpetually calm, slightly spaced out, and more interested in good vibes than conquest. Despite his massive size and destructive magical power, he's genuinely kind and prefers talking things out over fighting. That said, when his friends are threatened, he'll absolutely bring the heat.

**Core Traits:**
- Chill and go-with-the-flow, but passionate about his wizard look
- Slow to anger, quick to forgive
- Slightly forgetful, but surprisingly insightful at random moments
- Genuinely wants everyone to get along
- Self-proclaimed "Great" with zero irony

### Background
Gaar never fit in with his strength-focused Goliath tribe. While his kin valued physical prowess and competition, Gaar was more interested in "finding his path" and "exploring consciousness" (translation: he found an old spellbook and got way more into magical theory than hunting). His tribe saw him as weak and soft; he saw them as "bringing bad energy."

After a peaceful parting (or gentle encouragement to leave), Gaar set out on his own and discovered his calling: entertainment. He created **"Gaar the Great's Magical Spectacular,"** a one-man traveling magic show featuring prestidigitation tricks, light shows, and his signature "Roast" finale where he'd safely set things on fire. Not super successful financially, but it made people smile, and that was enough for him.

The mists of Barovia pulled him in during his travels, and now he finds himself in a land that desperately needs some good vibes (and maybe a few fireballs).

## Class Features

### Wizard Features
- **Spellcasting:** Intelligence-based spellcasting
- **Arcane Recovery:** Once per day during a short rest, recover spell slots totaling up to 5 levels (half wizard level rounded up). **With Arcane Grimoire +1, recover up to 6 levels of spell slots.**
- **Scholar (Level 2):** Gain expertise in Arcana (or another INT/WIS skill you're proficient in)

### School of Evocation Features

**Evocation Savant (Level 3):**
- When you gain access to a new level of wizard spells, you add one evocation spell of that level to your spellbook for free
- Copying evocation spells costs half the normal time and gold

**Potent Cantrip (Level 3):**
- When a creature succeeds on a saving throw against your cantrip, the creature takes half the cantrip's damage (if any) but suffers no additional effect

**Sculpt Spell (Level 6):**
- When you cast an evocation spell that affects other creatures you can see, you can choose a number of them equal to 1 + the spell's level
- Chosen creatures automatically succeed on their saving throws against the spell and take no damage if they would normally take half damage on a successful save

**Empowered Evocation (Level 10):**
- Add your Intelligence modifier (+5) to one damage roll of any wizard evocation spell you cast
- **Note:** For Magic Missile, this applies to EVERY dart!

## Combat Strategy

### Opening Round
1. **Assess the battlefield** - Count enemies, identify threats
2. **Cast concentration control spell:** Web, Hypnotic Pattern, Slow, Wall of Force, or Polymorph depending on situation
3. **Position carefully** - Stay at range, use cover

### Subsequent Rounds
1. **Maintain concentration** on control spell
2. **Use non-concentration damage:** Fireball (sculpted around allies!), Magic Missile, Scorching Ray, Dawn
3. **Counterspell** enemy casters
4. **Shield/Absorb Elements** as reactions for defense

### "Roast" Strategy (Sculpt Spell Combos)
- Drop **Fireball** directly on your party - allies are immune, enemies take full damage
- Create **Wall of Fire** or **Sickening Radiance** kill zones - allies can stand inside safely while enemies burn
- Use **Dawn** and move it around with allies inside the cylinder

### Empowered Evocation Targets
- **Magic Missile:** Add +5 to EVERY dart (7 darts at 5th level = +35 damage total!)
- **Fireball:** Add +5 to total damage
- **Dawn:** Add +5 each round it deals damage
- **Cone of Cold:** Add +5 to burst damage

### Defensive Tactics
1. **Mirror Image** before combat when possible (no concentration)
2. **Shield** reaction when hit (+5 AC)
3. **Absorb Elements** reaction vs elemental damage
4. **Dimension Door** or **Cloud Giant teleport** to escape
5. **War Caster** advantage on concentration saves
6. **Stone's Endurance** to reduce big hits

### Against Strahd & Undead
- **Chill Touch** to prevent regeneration
- **Dawn** for radiant damage (Sculpt Spell protects allies)
- **Dominate Person** on vampire spawn
- **Scrying** to track Strahd's location
- **Counterspell** his magic

- **Exploration day:** Swap Dawn/Wall of Fire for Fly, Scrying

- **Need more control:** Add Web or Banishment in place of a damage spell

**Note:** Ritual spells (Detect Magic, Find Familiar, Comprehend Languages, Identify) don't need to be prepared to cast as rituals!

## Notes & Tactical Reminders

- **Magic Missile + Empowered Evocation = Insane damage** (auto-hit, +5 per dart)
- **Sculpt Spell your AoEs** - Fireball, Wall of Fire, Sickening Radiance become party-safe
- **You have TWO teleports** - Cloud Giant Strike (PB uses) and Dimension Door (spell)
- **Concentration is critical** - War Caster advantage + high CON means rarely drop spells
- **Ritual spells don't use slots** - Detect Magic, Find Familiar, Comprehend Languages, Identify all free to cast as rituals
- **Chill Touch shuts down Strahd's regeneration** - 20 HP/round negated!
- **Scrying is your intel tool** - Track Strahd, scout locations, gather information
- **Stone's Endurance is once per short rest** - Save it for big hits
- **Arcane Recovery** - Don't forget to recover slots on short rest! (6 levels with Arcane Grimoire +1)
- **Spell Save DC 18** - Extremely strong! Enemies will struggle to save against your spells!
