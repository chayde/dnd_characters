# D&D 5e Build: The Wizard of the Roast

This build, titled **"The Wizard of the Roast"** by Colby from *d4: D&D Deep Dive*, is a high-performance, single-class Wizard build designed to maximize the "God Wizard" philosophy. It focuses on battlefield control, survivability, and devastating area-of-effect damage, utilizing the **Evocation** subclass to ensure allies remain unharmed while the Wizard reshapes the battlefield.

---

## Core Philosophy: The "God Wizard"
The "God Wizard" (a concept popularized by Treantmonk) doesn't focus on dealing the most damage personally. Instead, they focus on **battlefield control, buffing allies, and debuffing enemies**. By controlling the flow of combat, the Wizard ensures the party wins efficiently. This specific build adds a "Roast" element—using the **School of Evocation** to drop massive AOE spells (like *Fireball* or *Sickening Radiance*) directly onto the party without hurting them, thanks to **Sculpt Spells**.

## Character Creation

### Race
*   **Custom Lineage** (or Variant Human)
    *   **Feat:** **Resilient (Constitution)**. This is vital for maintaining Concentration on powerful control spells and boosting HP.
    *   **Variable Trait:** Darkvision.
    *   **Ability Score Increase:** +2 to Intelligence.

### Starting Stats (Point Buy)
*   **STR:** 8
*   **DEX:** 14 (For AC with Mage Armor)
*   **CON:** 15 (+1 from Resilient = 16)
*   **INT:** 15 (+2 from Race = 17)
*   **WIS:** 10
*   **CHA:** 8

## Level-by-Level Progression

### Level 1: The Foundation
*   **Class:** Wizard 1
*   **Key Spells:** *Shield*, *Absorb Elements*, *Sleep* (early game carry), *Find Familiar*.
*   **Strategy:** Stay back, use *Sleep* to end encounters instantly at low levels.

### Level 2: Subclass - School of Evocation
*   **Sculpt Spells:** This is the build's engine. You can create pockets of safety within your Evocation spells. You can now drop a *Thunderwave* or *Burning Hands* even if your fighter is in the middle of the pack.

### Level 4: First ASI
*   **Feat:** **Telepathic** or **Fey Touched** (+1 Intelligence).
    *   *Telepathic* is recommended for the +1 INT (reaching 18) and utility.
    *   *Fey Touched* is excellent for *Misty Step* and *Silvery Barbs* (if allowed).

### Level 6: Potent Cantrip
*   Your damage-dealing cantrips (like *Toll the Dead*) still deal half damage even if the target saves.

### Level 8: Capping Intelligence
*   **ASI:** +2 Intelligence (Reach 20 INT). Your spell save DC is now maximized.

### Level 10: Empowered Evocation
*   Add your Intelligence modifier (+5) to one damage roll of any Evocation spell. This makes *Magic Missile* significantly more powerful (adding +5 to *every* dart).

### Level 12: Concentration & Defense
*   **Feat:** **War Caster**. Between Resilient (Con) and War Caster, you will almost never drop concentration on your "God Wizard" control spells.

### Level 14: Overchannel
*   You can deal maximum damage with a spell of 5th level or lower. A max-damage *Fireball* or *Cone of Cold* can delete entire encounters.

## Tactical Advice

1.  **The "Sculpted" Kill Zone:** Use spells like *Sickening Radiance* or *Wall of Fire*. Because of **Sculpt Spells**, your melee allies can stand inside the radiance/fire, completely immune, while enemies are trapped inside and melting.
2.  **The Magic Missile "Gatling Gun":** At Level 10+, *Magic Missile* becomes a reliable source of high damage. Since it hits automatically and **Empowered Evocation** adds +5 to every dart, a 5th-level *Magic Missile* deals 7d4+7+35 damage (avg 59.5) with no save and no attack roll.
3.  **Concentration is Life:** Your primary job is to cast one big concentration spell (like *Slow* or *Wall of Force*) and then spend the rest of the fight using non-concentration spells (*Fireball*, *Magic Missile*, *Mind Sliver*) to finish the job.
4.  **Familiar Utility:** Use your Owl familiar for the "Help" action to give allies advantage, then have it fly away using "Flyby" to stay safe.
5.  **Overchannel Timing:** Save *Overchannel* for when you need to end a fight immediately. A max-damage *Cone of Cold* (8d8 = 64 damage to everyone in a 60ft cone) is a massive swing in momentum.
