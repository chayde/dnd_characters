# Vesper

> *"A vessel too small containing too much power. I am merely the glass through which the stars choose to shine."*

## Character Overview
Vesper is a Scourge Aasimar who serves as a living conduit for the terrifying, burning power of the cosmos. Standing barely four feet tall, they appear fragile and bird-like, yet they contain the searing energy of a dying sun. Their path is one of balance—maintaining their mortal shell while channeling the infinite, indifferent power of the stars.

*   **Class:** Druid (Circle of Stars)
*   **Race:** Scourge Aasimar (2024 Rules)
*   **Background:** Hermit
*   **Alignment:** Neutral Good
*   **Current HP:** 68
*   **Wisdom:** 20 (Max)

## Starry Forms
Vesper's connection to the heavens manifests in three distinct constellations:

*   **The Archer:** Unleashing radiant bolts as part of their spellcasting.
*   **The Chalice:** Maximizing the restorative power of their cosmic light.
*   **The Dragon:** Achieving unshakeable focus and a shimmering flight speed.
