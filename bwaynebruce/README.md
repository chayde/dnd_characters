# Bwayne

## Character Overview
Bwayne was once the "muscle" for a criminal organization known as The Vanished, breaking bones for coin in the shadows of Gray Haven. Haunted by his past, he found a new calling in the service of Hoar, the Doombringer. Now, he is a divine executioner of retribution, delivering poetic justice to those who believe themselves above the law.

*   **Class:** Paladin (Oath of Vengeance)
*   **Race:** Human (2024 Rules)
*   **Background:** The Vanished (Criminal/Enforcer)
*   **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
*   **Current HP:** 76
*   **Faith:** Hoar, God of Revenge and Poetic Justice
