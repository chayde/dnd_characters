## Character Overview
Chance McGinty is a jovial Lightfoot Halfling who treats life as one grand game of luck and skill. Traveling the roads of the world, he sets up a "Test Your Luck" contest for any stranger willing to engage in a friendly spar. Beneath his roadside charm lies the disciplined technique of the Open Hand, allowing him to control the flow of battle with speed and precision.

*   **Class:** Monk (Warrior of the Open Hand)
*   **Race:** Lightfoot Halfling (2024 Rules)
*   **Background:** Wayfarer
*   **Alignment:** Neutral Good
*   **Current HP:** 24
*   **Armor Class:** 16 (Unarmored Defense)

## Key Mechanics: The Luck Engine
Chance relies on a combination of halfling agility and pure fortune to survive:

*   **Halfling Luck:** Rerolling natural 1s ensures he rarely fails at the worst possible moment.
*   **Lucky Feat:** A pool of luck points allows him to turn the tide of any exchange.
*   **Open Hand Technique:** Controlling enemies by knocking them prone or stripping their reactions.
