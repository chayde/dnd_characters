# Klaus von Stauffer (Rune Knight) - Combat Cheatsheet

**Level 6** | AC 19 (Disadvantage to hit you) | HP 76 | Speed 30ft

---

## 1. START OF COMBAT (Turn 1)

### **BONUS ACTION: GIANT'S MIGHT**
*(3 Uses / Long Rest)*
- **Become Large:** You take up a 10x10 space.
- **Advantage:** On all Strength Checks and Strength Saves.
- **Extra Damage:** Deal **+1d6 damage** on one hit per turn.

### **ACTION: ATTACK (x2)**
- **Weapon:** +1 Maul (Topple)
- **To Hit:** +9
- **Damage:** 2d6 + 9 (+1d6 Giant's Might)

---

## 2. YOUR TURN - ACTION PRIORITY

### **Step A: ATTACK SEQUENCE**
1.  **Attack 1 (Maul):** Roll d20 + 9.
    *   **If Hit:** Deal 2d6 + 9 bludgeoning.
        *   **CRUSHER:** Push target 5ft (into hazard or away from ally).
        *   **TOPPLE:** Target makes **DC 16 CON Save** or falls **PRONE**.
        *   **FIRE RUNE (Optional):** Add 2d6 Fire + **Restrained** (DC 15 STR Save).
2.  **Attack 2 (Maul):** Roll d20 + 9.
    *   *(If target is Prone, roll with Advantage!)*
    *   **If Hit:** Deal 2d6 + 9 bludgeoning.
    *   **If Miss:** Deal 0 damage (unless you use Greatsword for Graze).

### **Step B: BONUS ACTION (If not using Giant's Might)**
- **Second Wind:** Heal 1d10 + 6 HP.
- **GWM Attack:** If you Crit or Kill, make one extra attack.

---

## 3. REACTIONS (The "No" Button)

**YOU CAN ONLY USE ONE REACTION PER ROUND. CHOOSE WISELY.**

| Trigger | Reaction Ability | Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Enemy CRITS you or Ally** | **CLOUD RUNE** (1/Short Rest) | Redirect the attack to an enemy. The enemy takes the Crit damage. |
| **Enemy hits you** | **Cloak of Displacement** | *(Passive)* They have Disadvantage. If they hit, effect turns off until your next turn. |
| **Enemy moves away** | **Opportunity Attack** | Attack them. Use **Crusher** to stop their movement or push them back. |

## 5. TACTICAL REMINDERS

*   **Cloud Rune is King:** Saving it for a Critical Hit is optimal, but using it to make a Boss hit its own minion (or vice versa) is also hilarious and effective.
*   **Fire Rune = Boss Killer:** Use this early on a boss. If they fail the **DC 15 STR Save**, they are **Restrained** (0 Speed, Advantage to hit them, Disadvantage on DEX saves).
*   **Crusher Control:** You can move people **5 feet** on a hit.
    *   Push them off a bridge.
    *   Push them into your Wizard's *Web* or *Wall of Fire*.
    *   Push them away so they can't attack you (if they lack reach).
*   **Topple + Action Surge:** If you knock an enemy Prone with Attack 1, **Action Surge** immediately to unload 3 more attacks with Advantage!
