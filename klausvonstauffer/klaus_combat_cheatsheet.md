# Klaus von Stauffer (Battle Master) - Combat Cheatsheet

**Level 6** | AC 19 (Disadvantage to hit you) | HP 76 | Speed 30ft

---

## 1. YOUR TURN - ACTION PRIORITY

### **Step A: MOVE & POSITION**
- **Ally in Danger?** Move next to them and use **BAIT AND SWITCH**.
    - **Cost:** 5ft movement + 1 Superiority Die.
    - **Effect:** Swap places. Add 1d8 to **your AC** (or theirs).
    - **Result:** You become an unhittable tank (AC 20-27 + Disadvantage).

### **Step B: ATTACK SEQUENCE**
1.  **Attack 1 (Maul):** Roll d20 + 9.
    *   **If Hit:** Deal 2d6 + 9 bludgeoning.
        *   **CRUSHER:** Push target 5ft.
        *   **TOPPLE:** Target makes **DC 16 CON Save** or falls **PRONE**.
        **MENACING ATTACK (Optional):** Add 1d8 dmg + **Frightened** (DC 16 WIS Save).
2.  **Attack 2 (Maul):** Roll d20 + 9.
    *   *(If target is Prone, roll with Advantage!)*
    *   **If Hit:** Deal 2d6 + 9 bludgeoning.

### **Step C: BONUS ACTION**
- **Second Wind:** Heal 1d10 + 6 HP.
- **GWM Attack:** If you Crit or Kill, make one extra attack.

---

## 2. REACTIONS (The "No" Button)

**YOU CAN ONLY USE ONE REACTION PER ROUND. CHOOSE WISELY.**

| Trigger | Reaction Ability | Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Enemy MISSES you** | **RIPOSTE** (1 Sup. Die) | **Attack them immediately.** Add 1d8 to damage. *(Best usage!)* |
| **Enemy hits you** | **Cloak of Displacement** | *(Passive)* They have Disadvantage. If they hit, effect turns off until next turn. |
| **Enemy moves away** | **Opportunity Attack** | Attack them. Use **Crusher** to stop their movement or push them back. |

---

## 3. SUPERIORITY DICE (4d8 / Short Rest)

**When to spend them:**
1.  **Riposte:** Use this whenever an enemy misses you. It's free damage.
2.  **Bait and Switch:** Use this to save an ally or when you are surrounded and need max AC.
3.  **Pushing Attack:** Use this to knock enemies off cliffs or away from squishy allies.

## 5. TACTICAL REMINDERS

*   **You are a Wall:** With Bait and Switch + Cloak of Displacement, you can easily reach **AC 24+ with Disadvantage**. Stand in doorways. Block monsters.
*   **Dread Lockdown:** Using **Menacing Attack** stops an enemy from moving closer to you. If you hit them and then step back (or use Crusher to push them), they are stuck and can't hit you with melee attacks.
*   **Topple First:** Always try to knock them Prone with your first attack so your second attack (and your allies) get Advantage.
*   **Tactical Mind:** Failed a Perception or Athletics check? Spend a **Second Wind** to add 1d10 to the roll. You rarely fail.
